Thank you for your interest in contributing to Geosaurus!

We’ll keep this simple, agile and open (within Esri, to start with).

Identify areas from the “Sub-projects” section in the [wiki](https://github.com/ArcGIS/geosaurus/wiki) where you’d like to contribute, based on your interest and experience.

Share this area with the group so everyone knows what others are working on and can help/collaborate, or pick another area for their own contributions. Multiple people can team up to work on an area as well. Coordinate and let everyone know. Mark your names on the wiki. We are using Slack for communicating - please see https://github.com/ArcGIS/geosaurus/wiki#Communication and join the Slack channel.

We’ll use a model of submitting pull-requests with our enhancements, issues will be reported and tracked on GitHub, and we’ll figure out the other stuff as we go along. Suggestions on how to improve our communication, collaboration and contributions are welcome!
